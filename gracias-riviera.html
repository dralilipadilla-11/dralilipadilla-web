How your consultation works

  1. Now You complete your questionnaire and upload any previous sleep studies or lab results.
  2. On the day of your appointment You join on Google Meet using the link in your email. The call is 45 minutes — joining five minutes early helps.
  3. During the call We review your case under the SER Protocol: Sleep, Balance and Respiration, together with your history and any studies you already have.
  4. After the call You leave with a clear picture of what your case actually requires — a sleep study, an airway evaluation, surgery, CPAP, or none of the above — and whether traveling to Cancún makes sense for you.

How to prepare

  • Have your previous sleep studies ready as PDFs, even if you already uploaded them.
  • Your current medication and supplement list, with doses and times. Include anything you take to sleep.
  • Data from your watch or sleep app, if you use one. It is useful context, not a diagnosis.
  • Join from a quiet place with a good connection, and use headphones if you can. We will discuss things that deserve privacy.
  • Test your camera and microphone beforehand. A computer is usually more stable than a phone.
  • If someone sleeps next to you and has noticed your snoring or pauses in breathing, their account is genuinely valuable. They are welcome to join the call.
